How grouping works Explainer for judges

  1. Every new alert is converted into a TF‑IDF vector.
  2. It's compared (cosine similarity) against every active, non‑expired incident.
  3. If the best match ≥ threshold → counter++ on that incident card.
  4. Otherwise → a brand‑new incident card is created.
  5. Incidents older than the cooldown window fade out and "reset" — the next similar alert starts a fresh card.
